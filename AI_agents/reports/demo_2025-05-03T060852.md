- **Strategy**: Investigate the purpose of bank transfers. If these are for savings, consider setting up automatic transfers. If for payments, ensure they're necessary and explore cheaper alternatives (e.g., free transfer services).

#### 5. **Cut Back on Dining Out**
- **Strategy**: Limit restaurant payments by meal planning, cooking at home more often, and using coupons or discounts for occasional dining out.
- **Actionable Tip**: Allocate a specific, reduced budget for dining out and track expenses closely to stay within limits.
